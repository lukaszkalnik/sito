# Slice 20 – Series without EXIF date

**Status:** ⬜ not started (moved from the roadmap; concept and plan when the slice starts)

## Idea

Photos dated only by the file (WhatsApp, downloads, copies) often share a copy or download time, so time alone is unreliable for them. Group them by time plus image similarity (embeddings from slice 19).

## Try

A folder of WhatsApp images forms only the series that really belong together.
