# Timeline and viewer

How the grid of photos and the photo viewer show photos and behave while they load, for galleries of tens of thousands of photos.

## Main window bar

- The bar above the grid holds, from left to right: „Sito”, a folder icon button („Wybierz inny folder”, its name and tooltip; disabled while a folder is being opened), a refresh icon button (↻, name „Odśwież”, tooltip „Sprawdź zmiany w folderze (F5)”; see [Files changed outside Sito](#files-changed-outside-sito)), the folder path and the summary (photos, series, photos without a date, unreadable entries), then the controls: „Odstęp w serii” (−, a number input in seconds, +), „Widok” and „Odrzucone zdjęcia do:” ([Rejecting](rejecting.md)), and „Cofnij” as an icon button (a curved arrow; its name stays „Cofnij”, its tooltip „Cofnij ostatnią zmianę (Ctrl+Z)”, in the viewer too).
- Each control has a small caption above it instead of a label beside it; the controls are aligned at the bottom, so „Cofnij” (no caption) lines up with them. Buttons and dropdowns share one look: a 1.5 px border, darker than the bar's separator line, 6 px corners, the height of the gap's −/+ buttons and an accent focus ring. Icon buttons (folder, „Odśwież”, „Cofnij”, the gap's −/+) draw their icon in the accent colour.
- „Widok” is a dropdown of the views („Serie”, „Wszystkie zdjęcia”), built from a list of views (`ui/src/features/timeline/views.ts`), so a new view adds one entry.
- Switching the view or the gap keeps the topmost visible photo in place, also at the very top: from „Serie” to „Wszystkie zdjęcia” the first series shown stays where it was, with the newer single photos above it.
- The bar is one row at every window width, so its height never changes, also not with background work.
- The path is cut in the middle: its start gives way first (with an ellipsis), so its last folder stays readable as long as possible (e.g. „C:\Users\Łukasz\Pic…\Wakacje nad morzem”). The full path is the tooltip.
- The summary line shows only the counts („34 296 zdjęć · 3600 serii”; just the photos while the series load). The full sentence („Znaleziono … Bez daty zdjęcia: 2. …”, also photos without a date and unreadable entries) is its tooltip, and what screen readers read.
- At the smallest window width (900 px) both lines are cut short; their tooltips hold the full text.
- Notes about background work float in a small pill at the bottom centre of the window, over the grid: „Sprawdzanie zmian…” while a reopened or refreshed folder is checked for changes, and the progress of the EXIF pass of a first scan ([Photos](photos.md#first-scan-of-a-takeout)); both can show together. Photos gone from disk get a note of their own there, with a button (see [Files changed outside Sito](#files-changed-outside-sito)). Clicks go through the pill to the tiles, and the grid ends with some empty space, so the last row can be scrolled above it. The undo toast shows at the top centre, just below the bar.

## Reopening a folder

- Sito reopens the folder opened last when it starts.
- **Of two openings that overlap, the later one wins.** This happens when the main window's page reloads while a folder is still being opened (the new page opens the last folder again). The earlier opening stops reading once the later one has begun, and if it finishes anyway its result is dropped; the later one is the folder shown, under its own scan id, so its thumbnails and the viewer keep working. The earlier page, now gone, gets no answer it would show (the backend says `superseded`, which the UI ignores). A refresh does not count as an opening: it never stops a folder that is being opened.
- Each folder reopens as it was left, whether it was closed by choosing another folder or by quitting Sito: with its own time gap ([Grouping](grouping.md)), in its view („Serie” / „Wszystkie”) and scrolled to the same place.
- The place is kept as the photo at the top of the grid (by its path in the folder) and how far its tile was scrolled past, not as a pixel offset, so it survives another window width, view or gap, and photos rejected or added since. If that photo is gone, the grid opens at the first older photo it shows, else at its last photo. A folder left at the very top opens at the top.
- The place is restored as soon as the grid shows photos, also from the cache before „Sprawdzanie zmian…” ends; the top photo then stays in place while the refresh changes the rows. If the user scrolls before that, the grid stays where they scrolled. It is stored when scrolling settles (also after the grid followed the viewer) and when the folder closes.
- View and place are kept for the 50 folders used last, on this machine only (webview storage).

## Files changed outside Sito

Nothing watches the disk: Sito notices changes when the user asks for a refresh, when a folder is opened, and when a photo fails to load. It never rescans on its own because of a failure.

- **„Odśwież”** (the ↻ button in the bar, or **F5** in either window) rescans the open folder in the background, like a reopened one: the grid stays usable, „Sprawdzanie zmian…” shows in the pill, and only new or changed files are read. It is disabled while a folder is being picked, scanned or refreshed (F5 then does nothing, and never reloads the window). A refresh stops the EXIF pass of a first scan, which goes on once the refresh is done.
- **Moved and renamed photos** are recognised by every scan (see [Photos](photos.md#moved-and-renamed-photos)): after a refresh they keep their place in both windows, so the viewer shows the same photo again and the grid keeps following it, and they keep their accepted mark ([Accepting](accepting.md)).
- **A photo whose file is gone** (moved, renamed or deleted outside Sito): its thumbnail or photo fails, and the pill shows „Niektórych zdjęć nie ma już w otwartym folderze – zostały przeniesione, zmieniły nazwę lub usunięto je.” with the button „Odśwież”, until a refresh starts. Tiles that failed load again whenever the timeline changes, so a moved photo's tile shows its thumbnail after the refresh.
- **The open folder itself is gone** (renamed, moved or its drive unplugged): a notice under the bar says „Otwartego folderu nie ma już na dysku – zmieniono jego nazwę, przeniesiono go lub odłączono dysk.” with the button „Wybierz inny folder”. The grid keeps showing the photos as they were, and „Odśwież” stays enabled, so a reconnected drive can be checked again; a refresh that finds the folder clears the notice. Choosing the folder under its new name opens it at once from the photo cache, with its accepted marks.
- Opening the last folder on start when it is gone ends in „Nie znaleziono wybranego folderu.”, as for any folder that is not there.

## Series in the grid

- Each series has a header with its photo count and time range (e.g. „3 zdjęcia · 17:00:11–17:00:22”); when it overlaps a series of another camera, the header also names its camera ([Grouping](grouping.md#several-cameras-at-one-event)). A series that fits on one row is a tinted block next to other blocks and single photos.

## Editing series

- A **context menu** on a tile (right-click, or the Menu key / Shift+F10 on a focused tile) lists the edits that fit the photo and do something the others do not ([Grouping](grouping.md#series-edited-by-hand)): „Podziel serię przed tym zdjęciem” (only on a photo in the middle of a series of three or more: before the last photo it would only take it out), „Wyjmij z serii” (in a series of three or more), „Dołącz do poprzedniej serii” / „Dołącz do następnej serii” (when the photo shown before / after it is in another series), then the edits of its whole series, „Rozdziel serię” and, for an edited series, „Przywróć automatyczne grupowanie”. A series of one or two photos only offers „Rozdziel serię”, as any of the three edits leaves single photos. On a single photo pinned by an edit the menu offers „Przywróć automatyczne grupowanie”. A photo with no edit to offer opens no menu.
- Right-clicking a **series header** lists the edits of the whole series.
- The menu is used by keyboard: the first entry has the focus, ↑/↓ move (wrapping around), Enter picks, Escape closes and gives the focus back to the tile; a click outside closes it too.
- A toast confirms each edit, with „Cofnij”: „Podzielono serię”, „Wyjęto <name> z serii”, „Dołączono <name> do serii”, „Rozdzielono serię”, „Przywrócono automatyczne grupowanie”. An edit that no longer fits (the series changed in another window) says so in an error toast.
- Undoing an edit („Cofnij” in either window, Ctrl+Z, or the toast's button) says what came back: „Cofnięto podział serii”, „Cofnięto wyjęcie <name> z serii”, „Cofnięto dołączenie <name> do serii”, „Cofnięto rozdzielenie serii”, „Cofnięto przywrócenie automatycznego grupowania”; if the log cannot be written: „Nie udało się cofnąć zmiany serii.” The grid scrolls to the newest photo that changed and pulses every changed photo (as a restored photo pulses; see [Grouping](grouping.md#series-edited-by-hand) for which photos count); with nothing visibly changed it does not scroll. The viewer keeps showing its photo; its strip follows the photo's series and pulses the changed photos in it.
- **Edited series** have a small pencil after their header text, with the tooltip and accessible name „Seria edytowana ręcznie – nie zmienia się z odstępem”. **Photos taken out of a series** (pinned on their own) carry a pencil in a round badge in the top-left corner of their tile, „Wyjęte z serii ręcznie – nie łączy się w serię z odstępem”.
- After an edit both windows follow, as after a rejection: the grid, the summary's series count and the viewer (its strip, previous/next).

### Editing series in the viewer

- **The viewer edits only what it shows, plus restoring.** It offers „Podziel serię przed tym zdjęciem”, „Wyjmij z serii” and „Rozdziel serię” on the shown photo and on any photo of the strip, under the grid's rules, and „Przywróć automatyczne grupowanie” on a photo of an edited series or a single photo pinned by an edit, exactly where the grid offers it. Joins stay in the grid: they move a photo into a series the viewer does not show. Restoring is the one edit that reaches photos outside the strip (photos taken out earlier, the other part of a cut, a neighbour joining at the gap); it is the other way back from an edit besides „Cofnij”, and asks nothing first.
- **A single photo or a series of one that was not edited has nothing to edit** in the viewer (the grid still offers „Rozdziel serię” on a series of one; whether that is a series at all is open in [slice 63](../slices/63-series-of-one.md)). An edited series of one and a pinned single photo offer only restoring.
- **Controls:** the icon-only „Edytuj serię” button (a pencil, tooltip „Edytuj serię (Shift+F10)”) before „Zaakceptuj” opens the menu of the shown photo below it; it is disabled, not hidden, with nothing to edit. A right-click on the photo opens its menu; a right-click on a strip thumbnail opens the menu of that photo without stepping to it. Shift+F10 (or the Menu key) opens the menu of the focused strip thumbnail, else of the shown photo below the button.
- **Direct keys:** P („Podziel serię przed tym zdjęciem”) and W („Wyjmij z serii”) act on the shown photo, once per press (a held key does not repeat them), with or without Shift. A key whose edit the photo does not offer (P on the first photo of a series, W on a single photo) does nothing. The menu shows the keys next to the two entries. A key pressed while an edit is still on its way is ignored.
- **While a menu is open only its keys act** (↑/↓, Enter, Escape), in the viewer as in the grid: Escape closes the menu, not the window, and arrows, Delete, A, P, W, Ctrl+Z and the zoom keys do nothing behind it. Escape gives the focus back to the opener (the button or the thumbnail).
- **After an edit the series stays in focus:** after „Wyjmij z serii” on the shown photo the viewer shows the photo of the strip after it (the one before it when it was the last), so W, W takes out two photos in a row; the taken-out photo is a single photo, reached with ←/→ in „Wszystkie”. After a cut the shown photo stays and the strip shows its new part. An edit from a thumbnail keeps the shown photo. After „Rozdziel serię” the shown photo stays as a single photo and the strip goes away. After „Przywróć automatyczne grupowanie” the shown photo stays and the strip shows its series as automatic grouping makes it at the current gap, pulsing the photos that look different now (as after „Cofnij”; the grid does not pulse after an edit).
- Toasts and errors are the grid's; „Cofnij” in the viewer undoes the viewer's edits like any other.
- **Marks:** the strip of an edited series starts with the pencil („Seria edytowana ręcznie – nie zmienia się z odstępem”); a photo pinned as a single photo shows the pencil after its name in the bar („Wyjęte z serii ręcznie – nie łączy się w serię z odstępem”). The menu and the marks use the viewer's dark colours whatever the system theme.

## Thumbnails in the grid

- Every tile is a landscape box. A landscape thumbnail fills it and is cropped to it; a portrait thumbnail (taller than wide, after the EXIF orientation) is shown whole, with the tile background at its sides. So a portrait file whose scene lies on its side (e.g. a sideways scan without an orientation tag) is recognisable as such; Sito does not turn it, since nothing in the file says how.
- A tile knows a thumbnail's shape once it has loaded; a thumbnail shown before keeps its shape when its tile comes back, without changing from cropped to whole.
- Thumbnails load in turns, at most 3 at a time (the backend renders 2 at once; an uncached thumbnail of a 10 MP photo takes about 0.2 s, longer on a USB hard disk):
  1. the tiles on screen, top to bottom and left to right;
  2. then, while nothing visible waits, the tiles the grid renders ahead off screen (4 rows above and below), nearest to the screen first. Loading ahead stops there.
- A tile takes its turn only after it has been rendered for 150 ms, so tiles that only fly past during fast scrolling never load. A tile that scrolls away before its turn never loads either.
- Thumbnails once shown appear at once when their tile comes back. After another folder is opened, thumbnails still queued for the previous one are dropped.
- Thumbnails are cached on disk (up to 500 MB, oldest removed first) and kept when the photo cache is emptied.

## Viewer

- The viewer loads one photo at a time, never the whole timeline: for the photo shown it gets the photo, the photos of its series (the strip), the previous and next photo of the view and the photo to show after rejecting it. The backend works these out from the series of the current gap, which it finds once per change of the timeline.
- Until the photo's details arrive, the viewer shows a spinner.
- The details of the previous and next photo are fetched ahead, together with the photos themselves, so stepping needs no wait. Fetched details are kept until the view (mode, gap) or the timeline changes.
- Stepping to another photo keeps the previous one for a moment, then shows a spinner if the new one is still loading.
- When the timeline changes (a rejection or undo in either window, a refresh, the EXIF pass), the viewer fetches the shown photo's details again, and a photo that failed to load is tried again.
- **A photo that fails to load** gets its reason from the backend: if its file is gone from the folder, „Tego zdjęcia nie ma już w otwartym folderze – zostało przeniesione, zmieniło nazwę lub usunięto je.” with the button „Odśwież folder” (the refresh of the main window, see [Files changed outside Sito](#files-changed-outside-sito)); if the whole folder is gone, „Otwartego folderu nie ma już na dysku. Wybierz go ponownie w oknie głównym.” (no button: choosing a folder belongs to the main window); otherwise „Nie udało się wczytać zdjęcia.” After a refresh, a moved photo shows again; one that is gone for good leaves the viewer as a rejected photo does: it moves on to the photo that took its place.
- **When the viewer's folder is no longer open** in the main window (another folder was opened there, or the same one again), the backend says so instead of sending a photo, and the viewer shows „Ten folder nie jest już otwarty w oknie głównym. Wybierz zdjęcie w oknie głównym.” in place of the photo and the spinner. A photo that is only not on the timeline yet (a restored one) keeps the spinner until the timeline changes.
- The photo fills the space between the bar and the strip (or the bottom edge, when it has no series) as large as it fits, uncropped, so a portrait photo fills the height. The webview applies the EXIF orientation, so a rotated photo is measured upright.
- At the right end of the bar, „Edytuj serię” (an icon button, see [Editing series in the viewer](#editing-series-in-the-viewer)) and „Zaakceptuj” (green outline, solid green when the photo is accepted; see [Accepting](accepting.md)) comes before „Odrzuć” (solid red), which is set about one button width apart from „Cofnij” and „Zamknij”, so the two are not hit by mistake. „Cofnij” (a curved arrow, as in the main window and in the empty viewer) and „Zamknij” (an ×, tooltip „Zamknij (Esc)”) are icon buttons; their names stay „Cofnij” and „Zamknij”.
- **Narrow windows:** at 824 px wide and below, „Zaakceptuj” and „Odrzuć” show only their icon (the check; the folder or trash), keeping their colours, names and tooltips, so the info keeps at least ~300 px next to „Edytuj serię” (measured with the widest label, „Cofnij akceptację”). The gap after „Odrzuć” stays at every width.
- **The bar's look** is the main window's shared controls ([Main window bar](#main-window-bar)) in their dark values, set on the viewer, so the viewer stays dark whatever the system theme: icon buttons draw their icon in the accent colour, „Dopasuj” in the zoom badge is a shared button, and „Zaakceptuj” and „Odrzuć” keep their own colours with the shared height, border width and corners.
- **The photo's info** takes two lines before the buttons, so the bar has one height (about 54 px) at every width and for every photo:
  - line 1: the name (bold, its own tooltip), the format badge („RAW”), then the folder with a small muted folder icon. The folder is cut in the middle like the main window's path, so its last folder stays readable longest; its tooltip names it („Folder: Takeout/Zdjęcia Google/Zdjęcia z 2023”) and screen readers hear „Folder:” before it. The photos at the top of the scanned folder show „folder główny”. In a narrow window the folder gives way first, then the name is cut with an ellipsis.
  - line 2: date and time, resolution, file size and the date source note („data z Google Takeout”), joined with „·”, cut at its end, with the whole line as tooltip.
- The **resolution** is the photo's as it is shown (after the EXIF orientation, e.g. „5472 × 3648”) and the **file size** is in binary megabytes with one decimal as Windows Explorer counts them („7,5 MB”; below 1 MB in whole kB). The resolution comes from the JPEG or TIFF header (nothing is decoded); RAW photos show only the file size, since most RAW files record no reliable size outside their per-camera headers.
- In the series strip, the current photo has a thick accent frame with a glow; the other photos are shown normally. Accepted photos carry a green check, in the strip as on their grid tiles. Stepping through a long series scrolls the strip just enough to keep the current photo visible.
- One click on a tile opens the viewer; there is only ever one viewer window, also when a tile is double-clicked. A viewer window that is still loading (or reloads) gets the last photo asked for from the backend.
- **The grid follows the viewer.** Whenever the viewer shows another photo (stepping, the strip, after a rejection or an undo), it tells the main window (`viewer-shown`), whose grid frames that tile with a steady accent frame (`aria-current`). The grid scrolls only when the photo is not fully visible, and then centres it; the scroll waits for a 150 ms pause, so holding an arrow key stays smooth, while the frame moves at once. A photo the grid does not show (e.g. a single photo while „Serie” is shown) gets no frame and no scroll. When the viewer window closes, however it is closed, the backend tells the main window (`viewer-closed`): the frame goes away, and the last photo shown is scrolled into view if needed and pulses for about 5 s (a restored photo pulses about 2.5 s). Both windows always show the same view, since the main window pushes its mode and gap to the viewer.
- **Double-clicks and held keys act once** where acting twice would harm: a double-click on „Zaakceptuj”, „Odrzuć”, „Cofnij”, „Zamknij” or a tile counts as one click (keyboard activation always counts), and holding A, Delete, P, W, Ctrl+Z or Escape does not repeat them. Held arrow keys (stepping, panning) and the zoom keys do repeat.

### Zoom

- The mouse wheel and a touchpad pinch zoom around the pointer. `+`/`-` zoom by ×1.25 around the middle of the view, `0` fits, `1` shows 100 %, and a double-click switches between fit and 100 % at the clicked spot. The webview's own page zoom never kicks in over the photo.
- 100 % means one photo pixel per screen pixel, also with Windows display scaling, so sharpness can be judged on real pixels. A RAW photo shows its embedded preview ([Photos](photos.md)), so there 100 % means the preview's pixels.
- The zoom goes from fit up to 800 %; zooming out past fit snaps back to fit. Fit is the photo as large as it fits, uncropped (a photo smaller than the view is enlarged to fit, as before zoom existed); a photo whose fit is already above 100 % cannot be zoomed further in by `1`.
- While zoomed, dragging pans the photo (grab cursor) and Shift+arrows pan by 120 pixels; plain arrows still step to the previous or next photo. The photo can't be moved past its edges, and a side smaller than the view stays centred.
- The position is kept relative to the photo (the spot in the middle of the view, as a fraction of its width and height), so resizing the window or stepping to a photo of another size keeps the same spot in view.
- Zoom and position carry over to the other photos of the same series, also after a rejection that shows the next shot of the series. Stepping into another series, or onto a photo without a series, resets to fit.
- While zoomed, a badge in the top-right corner shows the level (e.g. „100%”) and a „Dopasuj” button that goes back to fit. Nothing is shown at fit.

## Staying responsive

- The grid lays out its rows once, for its measured width, before it first shows them: opening a folder never builds the rows for a width the grid does not have and then replaces every tile, so a click on a tile right after opening always lands.
- Building the timeline and its series (seconds for tens of thousands of photos) never blocks the app: clicks, scrolling and thumbnails keep working while it reloads.
- During the background EXIF pass of a first scan ([Photos](photos.md#first-scan-of-a-takeout)) the timeline is reloaded at most every 30 s.
