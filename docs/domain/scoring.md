# Scoring (planned)

- Each photo of a series gets a weighted score from its [analysis](analysis.md) signals (sharpness, exposure, faces and eyes).
- The photo with the best score is **suggested** as the one to keep; the user always decides.
- The weights and thresholds are tuned in the app itself, not in a separate prototype: they are settings, and a debug panel shows each photo's individual scores.
